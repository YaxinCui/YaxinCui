Yaxin Cui

I am interested in evaluation, alignment, reasoning and engineering practice of large language models in vertical domains, with additional work on cross-lingual NLP, knowledge graphs, event mining and intelligent optimization algorithms.

当前关注大模型在垂直行业中的评测、对齐与推理，以及跨语言自然语言处理、知识图谱和智能优化算法的研究与工程落地。
